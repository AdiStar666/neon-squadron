2. Data within the app

Neon Squadron stores the progress and settings required for gameplay locally on your device. This may include mission and campaign progress, unlocked content, hangar and upgrade progress, selected ships or gameplay variants, as well as language, sound and other game settings.

This data is used to resume your game and retain your settings. The app does not transmit this local game save to us and does not require a separate Neon Squadron user account. The current test version does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Android device backups are separate from the app's local storage. Depending on your Android version, device settings and backup provider, local app data may be backed up or transferred to another device through operating-system features. These backup and transfer functions are provided by the operating system or the relevant provider; they do not give us direct access to your backups.

To the extent this local information constitutes personal data, it is processed to provide the game features you use, based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3. Distribution and closed testing through Google Play

Neon Squadron is distributed through Google Play and may be made available there as part of a closed test. Your Google Play account is used for downloading, installing, updating and participating in the test. Google may process account, device, connection, store and usage information as necessary to provide its services.

This processing is carried out by Google as part of its Google Play and account services. More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. No advertising, in-app purchases or additional tracking services

The current test version of Neon Squadron contains no advertisements, advertising SDKs, in-app purchases or subscriptions. It does not use separately integrated analytics or crash-reporting services operated for the developer and does not run a developer-operated server that collects gameplay or usage data.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game and settings data generally remains on your device until you reset it within the app, clear the app's data through Android, or uninstall the app. On Android, local app data can usually be removed through the system settings under “Apps → Neon Squadron → Storage → Clear data”; labels may vary by device.

Any backups or device transfers created by Android or another backup provider are managed separately through the settings of the relevant service.

6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process your email address, any name you provide and the contents of your message in order to respond. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual enquiries or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries. Our legitimate interest is handling questions and support requests relating to Neon Squadron.

We use Google's Gmail service for the contact address. Information about Google's processing, including international data transfers and safeguards, is available in Google's Privacy Policy. Contact enquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to handle them or to meet legal obligations.

7. Visiting this GitHub Pages website

This information page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub receives technical connection data. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor is signed in to GitHub. We use GitHub Pages to make this privacy policy securely and publicly available, based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

GitHub describes its processing of personal data, international transfers, safeguards and retention criteria in its Privacy Statement. Its GitHub Pages documentation explains IP-address logging. This page itself includes no analytics tools, advertising services, remote fonts or embedded third-party content and sets no cookies of its own. Language navigation uses section links without storing a preference.

8. Your rights

Where the legal conditions apply, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Where consent applies, you may withdraw it for the future. Contact us using the details above. We cannot access game saves stored exclusively on your device.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the EU member state of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

9. Changes

We update this privacy policy when Neon Squadron's features or data processing change. Before introducing advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or crash-reporting services, online accounts or other data-processing online features, this policy will be updated and any required consent will be obtained.
